OWL privacy policy

OWL (One Word Literature) for Meta Ray-Ban Display and Even G2 · Last updated November 15, 2026

The short version

OWL does not collect, send or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

OWL remembers your reading settings (speed, timing, words per flash, sound) and your place in each book, so you can pick up where you left off. This is kept in your device's local storage, never sent anywhere, and is erased when you remove the app.

What OWL loads

On Meta Ray-Ban Display, OWL loads its pages, books and narration recordings from this website. Like any website, the host's servers may keep standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for security and reliability. OWL itself does not read or keep them.

On Even G2, the books and recordings are packaged inside the app, so OWL makes no network requests at all.

Sensors and permissions

On Meta Ray-Ban Display, if you leave "When you walk" turned on, OWL reads the glasses' motion sensor while you're reading, only to notice when you start walking and step the words aside. The readings are used on the spot and never stored or sent. You can turn this off in Settings.

OWL does not use the camera, microphone or location on any device.

Children

OWL collects no information from anyone, including children.

The books and recordings

The stories in OWL are in the public domain. Narrations come from LibriVox volunteers, whose recordings are also in the public domain.
